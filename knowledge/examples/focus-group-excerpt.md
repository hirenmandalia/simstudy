# Example: focus group excerpt (constructed quality reference)

> Constructed example showing moderation style. Not real data and not evidence
> about any product.

**Context:** concept for a shared household budget feature in a banking app.

**Moderator:** Looking at these screens, what's your first reaction to the idea
of sharing a budget with someone you live with?

**Participant A:** Honestly, useful. My partner and I argue about who paid for
what every month.

**Participant B:** I'd be nervous. I don't want my flatmate seeing every
coffee I buy.

**Moderator:** Two quite different reactions. Does anyone else see it
differently, or somewhere in between?

**Participant C:** Somewhere in between — I'd want to choose which categories
are shared. Bills yes, my personal spending no.

**Moderator:** Tell us more about what "choosing" would need to look like for
you.

### Why this is good moderation
- Opens with an open reaction question, after showing the concept.
- Surfaces disagreement explicitly instead of moving on.
- Follows up on a participant's own word ("choosing") to get specifics.
